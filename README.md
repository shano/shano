### Hi, I'm Shane

Platform Engineering Manager at [Syntasso](https://www.syntasso.io/), based in London. I co-host the [Hybrid Cloud Show](https://hybridcloudshow.com), a podcast on Kubernetes, cloud infrastructure, homelabs and platform engineering. I also write the odd post over at [shanedowling.com](https://shanedowling.com/).

Most of my code lives on my own Gitea these days, running on [my homelab](https://shanedowling.com/homelab/). These are the few things I maintain in the open:

**Apps**

- [assistral](https://github.com/shano/assistral): a simple Android wrapper for Mistral's Le Chat
- [harbour-sailreel](https://github.com/shano/harbour-sailreel): NewPipe-style video and audio streaming for Sailfish OS
- [Katapult](https://github.com/shano/Katapult): my fork of the Katapult grid launcher for Android, it adds an indicator showing whether you're on a VPN

**Scripts and lists**

- [awesome-backstage](https://github.com/shano/awesome-backstage): a curated list of Backstage resources
- [email-to-remarkable-sync](https://github.com/shano/email-to-remarkable-sync): send PDFs to a mailbox and they turn up on your reMarkable ([write-up](https://shanedowling.com/posts/email-to-remarkable/))
- [dumbphone](https://github.com/shano/dumbphone): scripts to make your smartphone a little dumber ([write-up](https://shanedowling.com/posts/make-your-smartphone-a-little-dumber/))

Issues and PRs welcome on any of them.
